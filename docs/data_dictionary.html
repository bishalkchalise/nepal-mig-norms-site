Destination controls {#controls}

Share-weighted, lagged two years, standardised across the district × year panel, one set per channel (dc_<var>_<channel>): gdp_pc (log GDP per capita, constant USD), unemp_total, female_lfp, parl_female (women in parliament, %), fertility (TFR), educ_spend (% GDP), resource_rent (% GDP). Source: WDI via data/clean/migration/destination_controls.rds. Population: district_pop_2011.csv (2011 Census); LG population 2021 for INSEC offsets (pop_lg, lpop).

DHS men's recode {#dhs-men}

mwb_goesout … mwb_burnsfood, mwb_any (mv744a–e), mwb_index (derived); controls age, educ_years, urban, wealth_q; weight mv005/10⁶; district from smdist/smdistr.

Household roster {#dhs-roster}

Children aged 5–17 of the respondent (matched on cluster × household × mother line): attendance, completed years, by sex — aggregated to the mother as above.

INSEC {#insec}

sv68_panel.csv (LG × year, 68 non-office districts): sv_all (rape + attempted rape + sexual abuse), sv_women (18+), sv_child (<18), sv_rape, sv_attempt, sv_abuse, other_all (non-gendered: killing, beating, injured, arrest, assembly, racial — reference category), pop_lg, lpop, urban (Nagarpalika/metropolitan = 1), province, district. Years 2020–2025 (2018–19 geocode incompletely).

Courts {#courts}

court_taxonomy_panel.csv (district × year, 2016–2025, Nepali subject taxonomy): divorce_all, alimony_all, marital_pkg, partition, rape, dv, polygamy, ref_commercial (cheque dishonour, lending, banking, fraud, forgery — reference category), unclassified, n_total, pop, lpop, province.
