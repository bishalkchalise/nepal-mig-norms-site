DHS violence harmonisation — Nepal 1996–2022

Availability was established by loading the cleaned women's/men's recodes (mig-gendered-norms/data/clean/dhs/np_dhs_all_rounds_{women,men}.rds, data/clean/analysis/analysis_{women,men}.rds) and counting non-missing values by round, and by reading the extraction code (script/var/01_dhs_women.R, 02_dhs_men.R). "Available" below means the item was asked to a comparable universe with comparable coding — not merely that a DHS file exists.

Round sizes (women's recode): 1996 8,429 · 2001 8,726 · 2006 10,793 · 2011 12,674 · 2016 12,862 · 2022 14,845. 1996 has no district identifier and is dropped from every analysis. The 1996 and 2001 women's recodes interview ever-married women only; 2006+ interview all women 15–49.

D. Wave rule applied in the clean pipeline

  • Attitudes (women, men): 2001, 2006 | 2016, 2022.
  • IPV experience and controlling behaviour: 2016, 2022 (post-only; 2011 optional, not preferred).
  • Agency: 2001, 2006, 2011, 2016, 2022.
  • Indices are rebuilt inside each estimation sample so that no wave with a gated or missing item contributes padded zeros.
