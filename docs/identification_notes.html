Identification notes

The object being estimated

Both papers estimate reduced-form shift-share regressions of the Godlonton–Theoharides form:

Y_ipt = β Z_pt + γ W̃_pt + X'θ + α_p + δ_t + ε_ipt,   Z_pt = M_p × W̃_pt,
W̃_pt = std[ Σ_d s_pd W_{d,t−2} ].

M_p (baseline intensity) is absorbed by the district effects; W̃_pt enters in levels so that β is identified from the interaction of how migration-intensive a district is with how much its destination mix's index moved. β is the change in Y per additional permit per 1,000 residents at a one-SD higher destination index two years earlier.

Two ways to justify it

Shares view (Goldsmith-Pinkham, Sorkin & Swift 2020). The destination shares are the instrument; they must be uncorrelated with district-level shocks to Y conditional on FE and controls. Nepal's shares reflect historical recruitment networks; the main threat is that districts that send migrants to, say, Malaysia differ in trends from those sending to Qatar for reasons other than Malaysian legal reform. The Diagnostics page therefore shows balance of 2001 DHS district characteristics on exposure, leave-one-destination-out estimates and the destination-contribution decomposition.

Shocks view (Borusyak, Hull & Jaravel 2022). Destination index changes must be as-good-as-random across destinations and numerous enough. Nepal's corridor is concentrated (effective number of destinations ≈ 3–5 per district; Malaysia, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait dominate), so the shocks view is weak here — there are few independent shocks per year. This is the main reason we do not report BHJ/AKM shock-level inference and instead lean on the shares view, the lead-shifter placebo and the within-unit-year stacked design for administrative counts.

Threats, in order of importance

  1. Destination macro conditions. Legal-equality and anti-VAW indices co-move with destination development; migrant earnings and labour demand change at the same time. Full block adds share-weighted destination GDP, unemployment, female LFP, parliament, fertility, education spending and resource rents (lagged, per channel).
  2. Differential regional trends. Region × linear trend in the Full block. District linear trends are deliberately not offered: with four to five waves they absorb most of the identifying variation, and the legacy project found that "everything dies under district trends" for the admin families.
  3. Reporting and caseload effects (admin families). Level Poisson designs failed their placebos repeatedly in the legacy analysis (male-victim and non-gendered incidents moved too). The stacked design compares the gendered category with a reference category within unit × year, absorbing anything that moves the whole caseload. Stacked is the default; level with offset is kept as an alternative.
  4. Anticipation / trends correlated with the destination mix. Lead placebo: the exposure rebuilt with W_{t+2} should not predict Y.
  5. Measurement breaks. 2011 DHS attitude items gated by a Nepal-specific screener; 2011 "ever" sexual-violence items fielded differently; INSEC subtype codes appearing mid-panel; 29% of court cases missing the English label. Each is handled by exclusion or by using the stable classification (Nepali labels; stable INSEC subtypes).
  6. Concentration. Leave-one-out of the top eight destinations; Var(Z) decomposition.
  7. Thin female channel. In the 2009–2011 window 25 of 75 districts have fewer than 50 female permits; the 2009–2012 default and the 2012–2015 / 2009–2015 windows are offered. Female-channel estimates remain imprecise; the channel-equality test's conclusion depends on the scaling (see HANDOVER §3a) — the IQR-scaled magnitudes are stored alongside every estimate.

What is not claimed

No result on this site is labelled causal, robust or confirmed by the interface. The estimator labels are "reduced form", "2SLS", "stacked Poisson", etc. The 2SLS option instruments the only household treatment observed in every wave (husband currently away); its first stage is weak (F < 1) because the destination index is not a migration-inducing shock, so 2SLS coefficients are shown for completeness only.
