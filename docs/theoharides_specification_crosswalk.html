Godlonton & Theoharides (2025) — the direct template

Y_imt = β0 + β1 MigRate_m0 + β2 LibIndex_{m,t−2} + β3 MigRate_m0 × LibIndex_{m,t−2} + β4 DestShare_m0 + δ_p + τ_t + δ_p·t + ε, with shares in 1992, index lagged two years and standardised, province FE, year FE and province linear trends, province-clustered SEs. Our equation is the same with MigRate_m0 absorbed by district FE, LibIndex → WBL/UN-VAW, trends = region linear trends (Full block), clusters = district.

Scaling rule adopted (W.5 of the brief)

M_p = annual-average non-India permits per 1,000 residents over the window (so a 7-year window is on the same scale as a 3-year one); W̃ standardised across the district × year panel 2001–2026 within window × channel × shifter. Coefficients are per unit of Z; the magnitude β × IQR(M_p) is stored with every estimate and is the quantity to compare across windows.
